Sources & methodology

Rates are from Statistics Canada life tables for 2022–2024 (three-year averages): Table 13-10-0114-01 (Canada and nine provinces, single-year ages) and Table 13-10-0140-01 (Prince Edward Island, Northwest Territories, Nunavut; 5-year age groups converted to annual rates assuming a constant risk within each group; ages 90–100 extrapolated).

Yukon sex-specific tables are not published, so national rates are shown. Statistics Canada does not publish full life tables below the province/territory level.

These are population averages, not a personal prediction. Individual health, income and lifestyle matter a great deal.
